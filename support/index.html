Help & support

Contact us

Email support@fitologika.com. We reply within two business days.

Common questions:

  • Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot password?” and follow the email link.
  • Change your calorie or weight goals? Profile → Goals → Edit, or Recalculate.
  • Switch between lb and kg? Profile → Units.
  • Reminders not arriving? Allow notifications for Fitologika in your phone's Settings, then turn reminders on in Profile.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Profile → Your data → Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm. Deletion is immediate.

Without the app: email support@fitologika.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: your account and sign-in details, profile and goals, food log, water, weigh-ins, and any questions you sent to providers.

What is kept: anonymous daily click counts on marketplace listings, which never contain personal information. If you also ran a vendor account, your published business listings stay up without your personal details until removed by our team.
