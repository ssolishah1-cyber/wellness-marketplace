Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

This policy explains what Love Angel Healthcare LLC ("we") collects when you use this nutrition app, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and an encrypted password.
  • Health and nutrition data you enter: food you log, calories and nutrients, water, weigh-ins, and your goals. If you use the setup wizard, the age, height and sex you enter are used once to calculate suggested targets and are not stored.
  • Health focus (optional): if you choose one, such as blood sugar, blood pressure, IBS, focus or stress & sleep, we store it only after you give consent, and use it only to tailor tips, meal ideas and workouts in the app. Remove it or withdraw consent at any time in Profile.
  • Preferences and workouts: food preferences and allergies, fitness level, where and how often you work out, and the workouts you mark as done.
  • Questions you send to providers in the Market: the name, email and message you choose to send, only after you tick the consent box.
  • Marketplace clicks: a daily count of how often each listing is opened. These counts contain no information about who clicked.

Subscriptions: if you buy a plan, Apple or Google process the payment; we receive your plan, renewal dates and status (never your card details) through our payment provider RevenueCat. If you claim the free book with the Max plan, we store the shipping address you give us, only to send the book.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your log, progress, goals and coaching tips.
  • To keep your account secure and to send account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password resets.
  • To deliver a question to a provider when you ask us to.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal or health data.
  • We never use your meals, weight, goals, health focus or other health data to choose, rank or target marketplace listings or ads.
  • We never share your health data with marketplace partners, including affiliated partners such as TriggerMD and XIMMD.

Who can see your data

You can see all of your data when signed in. Our care team (staff with admin access at Love Angel Healthcare LLC) can also view your profile, goals, health focus, food, water, weight and workout logs, so they can support you, answer questions and improve the service. Every time a staff member opens a client's record it is logged, and access is limited to authorised staff. Staff who review Market listings cannot see client data.

Your data is stored with our hosting providers (Supabase for the database and sign-in, Vercel for the website), who process it on our behalf. A provider you contact through the Market sees only the details you chose to send.

Sponsored and affiliated listings

Some Market listings are sponsored or come from businesses affiliated with us. They are always labelled. Links to partner websites tell the partner the visit came from this app, but never include your name or health data. Purchases and services on partner sites are covered by their own privacy policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data at any time from Profile.
  • Delete your account from Profile. This permanently removes your account, food log, water, weight, workouts, goals, health focus and questions you sent.
  • Correct your goals and entries in the app at any time.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules ensure each person can only access their own health data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you as required by law.

Children

The app is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Not medical advice

Coaching tips, condition guides, meal ideas, workouts and suggested targets are general information, not medical advice or treatment. Talk to a qualified clinician about your health.

Changes

If we make material changes, we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
